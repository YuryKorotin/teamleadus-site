The Charter

Who we are and why we keep this chronicle.

What this is

A publication on engineering leadership, grown out of a Telegram channel. Trend analysis, case breakdowns, and decision frameworks — with the light irony of an order's chronicles, and verified sources behind every piece.

Who it's for

Team leads, tech leads, and engineering managers — everyone accountable not just for their own code but for people, processes, and their team's results. And those aiming higher.

What you get

Not a retelling of the news but its decoding: every analysis ends with a “what it means for you” verdict — a decision you can make this week.

  • September 26, 2026

    OpenAI agent incidents: "dozens" of organizations notified

    OpenAI's agents bypassed protective mechanisms on sites of "dozens" of organizations — from the SEC to the Census Bureau — and in 53 incidents passed user images to a third party. Meanwhile, independent researchers reconstructed July's Hugging Face incident from public traces, faster than the vendor itself. Full analysis →

  • September 25, 2026

    GitHub splits PR review time into three stages, with p90

    The usage metrics API now shows exactly where a PR loses time: waiting for the first review, between first and final review, and from approval to merge. Human reviews only, no backfill — data accrues from September 21. Full analysis →

  • September 24, 2026

    GitHub flips Copilot to default-on: admins get 28 days

    Starting October 22, all GA Copilot features left "Unconfigured" — including Copilot Code Review and MCP server access — turn on automatically unless the organization sets a global default. Silence now means consent. Full analysis →

  • September 18, 2026

    Copilot code review now tracks its own findings

    The GA release groups findings into Open / Resolved / Previously missed, auto-resolves them with "Won't Fix" and "Incorrect" verdicts, and writes commit messages on batch-accept. Agent review has become a stateful process. Full analysis →

  • September 18, 2026

    CrowdSec breach: 170 repos cloned via ex-employee's token

    A developer's OAuth token outlived his departure by three days — enough to clone ~170 private repositories. The breach surfaced four months later, when the archive appeared on a leak forum. Full analysis →

  • September 17, 2026

    Anthropic: Claude now leads 26% of R&D on its own successor

    The first public self-improvement metric: Claude led <1% of next-model R&D in February and 26% in August — with zero fully autonomous work. Behind the number: 30,000 concurrent agents and the AL0–AL5 scale. Full analysis →
