The Charter

Who we are and why we keep this chronicle.

What this is

A publication on engineering leadership, grown out of a Telegram channel. Trend analysis, case breakdowns, and decision frameworks — with the light irony of an order's chronicles, and verified sources behind every piece.

Who it's for

Team leads, tech leads, and engineering managers — everyone accountable not just for their own code but for people, processes, and their team's results. And those aiming higher.

What you get

Not a retelling of the news but its decoding: every analysis ends with a “what it means for you” verdict — a decision you can make this week.
