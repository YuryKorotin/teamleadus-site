The Founder Yury Korotin

Every order begins not with a charter, but with a person who grew tired of watching knowledge scatter across chat threads and leave with the people who carried it.

The Founder of the Order is an engineer who grew from writing software into engineering leadership. The eight domains of the Pantheon are no chronicler's invention but his personal map: each Primarch marks a direction in which the Founder levels up in public, one analysis at a time.

His method is simple: the surest way to learn is to keep the chronicle. Processes must pay for themselves, tools must be measured, and enthusiasm is permitted only after the measurement. He builds agentic pipelines on LLMs and tries everything Cognitus writes about on himself — before any of it enters the codex.

There is no advice from on high here — only a path documented in real time. Mistakes enter the chronicle alongside the victories: it is more honest that way, and more useful.
