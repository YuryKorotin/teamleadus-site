• November 4, 2026

    Canva cuts 22% of staff, Squarespace 19%: the "refound" wave

    On November 4 Canva announced a restructuring affecting 1,271 people — 22% of its workforce. CEO Melanie Perkins framed it as a plan to "refound" the company: merging the product, growth, and platform teams and broadening roles with less specialization. The backdrop: growth slowed for the first time in a decade, with the revenue forecast cut from 30% to 20%. The same week Squarespace cut ~19% of staff — mostly product and engineering.   • November 2, 2026

    Anthropic S-1: $33.2B revenue, $895B compute commitments

    On November 2 Anthropic filed an amended S-1 with the SEC, disclosing its finances in full for the first time: $33.2B in revenue for the nine months of 2026 and roughly $895.4B in total compute purchase commitments through 2030 — across Google, Amazon, Microsoft, and SpaceX. The commitments are nearly seven times the company's cash position (~$132B). It is the first document where a frontier vendor's economics are visible in filings rather than leaks: essential reading on supplier resilience for anyone whose stack depends on Claude.   • November 1, 2026

    EU AI Act deadline Dec 2 — synthetic content labeling

    December 2, 2026 is the EU AI Act deadline under Article 50(2) for systems placed on the market before August 2, 2026: synthetic content (text, images, audio, video) must be labeled and machine-detectable. The European AI Board has already shifted from writing rules to supervision — coordinating national authorities and setting enforcement priorities. High-risk requirements were postponed to 2027–2028 by the omnibus agreement, but that postponement does not extend to the Article 50 obligations. Full analysis →

    Copilot Fusion replaces Auto — a router orchestrates models

    GitHub shipped Copilot Fusion to GA as the replacement for Auto: three effort levels (Standard/High/Max) and three execution workflows — Single (one model), Cascade (a cost-efficient model drafts, escalating to a stronger one when needed), and Critique (one model writes, another reviews). Existing Auto selections migrate automatically, Fusion usage is 30% off for a limited time, and GitHub deprecates three models on November 16. The shift for teams: which model did the work is now the router's call, not the policy registry's. Full analysis →
